# RQ3 (network part): Collaboration communities

**Method.** Nodes are the 2,489 EU institutions in the candidate set. Two institutions are linked when they appear on the same paper; the link weight is the number of shared papers. This gives 13,408 links (density 0.4%). Centrality (weighted degree, degree, approximate betweenness) and Louvain communities (weighted, seed 42) were computed on the largest connected component, which contains 2,124 institutions (85.3%); 279 institutions have no EU co-author and are isolated. Communities were compared with a just-country baseline on the same graph.

**Centre of the network.** CNRS is the main hub: 455 weighted links to 263 partners and the highest betweenness (0.11, more than twice the next institution). Inserm (304), LMU Munich (220), the University of Bonn (210) and Sorbonne Université (187) follow. Bonn and Leibniz University Hannover have high weighted degree with few partners (69 and 27), which indicates repeated collaboration among the same institutions. Betweenness also highlights bridging institutions such as Karolinska Institutet, TU Munich, Bologna, Athens and Porto.

**One community or several?** Several. Louvain finds 17 communities, 14 with at least 30 institutions. Country shapes them strongly: the French-led community is 90% French, the German-led 75%, Portuguese 71%, Spanish 67%. Others are mixed: the Dutch-led community is 32% Dutch and the Finnish-led one 23% Finnish, joined by Nordic and Benelux institutions. The Louvain partition has modularity 0.59 against 0.50 for the partition by country, and 38% of link weight crosses a national border. Collaboration is therefore national-regional, not purely national. The most frequent country pairs are Germany-Italy (76 joint papers), Germany-Netherlands (76) and Spain-Italy (73).

**Robustness.** Using only "Likely relevant" papers, dropping papers with more than 10 institutions, or keeping only repeated links gives 15 to 17 communities, modularity of 0.59 to 0.75 (always above the country baseline) and CNRS, Inserm and the University of Bonn in the top five by weighted degree in every variant.

**Limitations.** The candidate set is not manually validated, and Inserm, Karolinska and the Charité among the hubs suggest some health-science papers are included. Louvain is a heuristic and depends on seed and resolution. A link means a shared paper, not close collaboration. Umbrella organisations such as CNRS and Inserm group many laboratories under one name, which inflates their centrality. OpenAlex institution names may be split or merged.
